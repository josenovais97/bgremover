This extracts text, not layout

A PDF doesn't contain paragraphs — only glyphs at coordinates — so structure has to be inferred. You get the prose in order, with headings and page breaks. You don't get the original's columns, tables or exact spacing. The preview below is the real output.

What this does well — and what it doesn't

Good at prose

Reports, letters, contracts, essays — running text comes across cleanly, with hyphenated line breaks rejoined and headings kept as headings.

Weak at layout

Multi-column pages, tables and forms lose their arrangement. That's a limit of the format, not of this tool — no converter reads a PDF's structure, because there isn't one.

Never uploaded

Every other PDF-to-Word site wants your file on their server. This one reads it in your browser and sends nothing.
