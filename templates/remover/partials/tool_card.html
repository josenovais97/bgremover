Every thumbnail is lazy with intrinsic dimensions: these blocks sit below the fold, so none of this competes with the hero or moves LCP. Where a before/after pair exists, the "before" cross-fades in on hover or keyboard focus — the transformation is the pitch. Tools without artwork simply render without the image rather than showing a placeholder.
