{% load i18n_extras %} {% comment %} One tool as a compact card: icon, name, one line of what it does. Shared by tool_grid.html (the homepage's full set) and related_tools.html (the handful cross-linked at the foot of every page) so the two cannot drift. This used to lead with a large photograph of the tool's result. The idea was right and the execution could not hold: a demo image only sells a tool when it shows the TRANSFORMATION, and for most of these the honest artwork is just a photo. A golden retriever illustrated both "Crop" and "Resize"; a zebra stood in for "Remove object" and "EXIF". Recycled subjects across thirty-three cards read as stock filler, and the grid alone made the landing page over 8,000px tall — so the thing that was meant to show the toolkit off was mostly what made it tiring to look at. The demo images have not been thrown away; they still lead each tool's own page, where they sit beside that tool's controls and are contextual rather than decorative. Here, the name and the verb do more work in a tenth of the height. `item` carries `accent_vars` (from the context processor), which re-points the accent tokens for this subtree; `.tool-card` re-derives the text pair from them. Everything inside then uses the ordinary accent utilities — `bg-primary/10`, `text-primaryText` — and resolves to THAT tool's colour in both themes.
