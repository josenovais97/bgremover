The service

ClearBG provides free, browser-based image tools (background removal, cropping, conversion, and more). The tools run on your device; we don't host or store your files. The service is provided free of charge for personal and commercial use.

Because the processing happens in your browser rather than on our servers, there is no account to create, no per-image quota to exceed, and no processing queue. It also means the service depends on your device and browser: an older phone, a browser with scripting restricted, or a device short on memory may be slower or unable to run the tools that need an AI model. Where that happens the page will say so rather than fail silently, and the tools that need no model generally keep working.

Exports are full resolution and carry no watermark. We do not add a paid tier to the export step, and we do not restrict output quality by usage. Should that ever change, it would apply to future use of the service and not retroactively to work you have already produced.
